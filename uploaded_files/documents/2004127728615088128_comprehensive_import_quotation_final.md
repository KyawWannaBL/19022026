# Subject: Quotation for Importation Customs Clearance and Duties

Further to your request, BRITIUM EXPRESS is pleased to provide this detailed and comprehensive quotation for the customs clearance, duties, taxes, and associated service charges for the importation of three distinct product consignments: Scarves, Hats, and Glass Beads.

This document provides a transparent, line-by-line breakdown of all government-levied charges for each product, followed by a summary of our consolidated service fees and the final grand total payable. All calculations are based on the provided CIF values and prevailing tariff rates.
