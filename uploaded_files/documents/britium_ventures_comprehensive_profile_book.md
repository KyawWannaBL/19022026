## Product Highlight: Flexitanks for Liquid Logistics

Engineered for maximum efficiency in bulk liquid transport, our advanced flexitank solutions represent a paradigm shift for industries dealing with non-hazardous liquids. They offer a significantly more cost-effective, safe, and environmentally friendly alternative to traditional transport methods like steel drums or Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBCs).

## What is a Flexitank?

A flexitank is a large, flexible bladder-like container that is fitted inside a standard 20-foot shipping container. Once filled, it transforms the container into a safe and efficient bulk liquid transport unit. After the liquid is discharged, the flexitank is removed and can be recycled, allowing the container to be used for standard dry cargo.

## Domestic Delivery Rates (Myanmar)

We believe in transparent and competitive pricing. The following rates apply to our express parcel delivery service within Myanmar's key economic hubs. Our pricing is structured by region and, within Yangon, by clearly defined zones to provide fair value.

All standard delivery rates listed cover parcels with a weight of up to 5 Kg.

## Overweight Surcharge Policy

For parcels exceeding the 5 Kg base weight, an additional surcharge of +500 MMK per additional 1 Kg will be applied. The weight is rounded up to the next kilogram.

## Calculation Examples:

A 4 Kg parcel to Sanchaung (Yangon Zone A): 3,000 MMK (Base Rate). ✓

A 7 Kg parcel to Sanchaung (Yangon Zone A): 3,000 MMK (Base Rate) + (2 kg × 500 MMK) = 4,000 MMK. ✓

A 10 Kg parcel to Mandalay: 5,000 MMK (Base Rate) + (5 kg × 500 MMK) = 7,500 MMK. ✓
